Writing things down is the best way I know to find out whether I actually understand them. This blog is where I'll put research write-ups, paper notes and small findings from my work on LLMs, search and ranking.

Expect three kinds of posts:

- **Research.** Long-form write-ups of experiments, with code and results.
- **Notes.** Condensed readings of papers and topics.
- **Findings.** Short posts with one plot, one result or one gotcha.

If something here is wrong, please tell me. That's half the point.
