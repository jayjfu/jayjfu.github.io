ML engineer · Search, ranking & LLMs

I build systems that find, rank and reason.

I'm Jay Fu, a machine learning engineer. I spent four years on web and video search, ranking and recommendation at Apple and BIGO, and I'm now finishing an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at San José State University.

These days I focus on three areas: training and post-training large language models, especially for coding and personalization; retrieval and ranking; and vision-language models as teachers for small, fast detectors. I write here about what I build and what I learn along the way.
